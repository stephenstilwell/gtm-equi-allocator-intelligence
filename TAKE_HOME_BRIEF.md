# Agentic GTM Lead | First working version

## Hypothesis

An RIA or multi-family office with a verifiable, timely external-manager or alternative-investment buying signal is a more useful prospect for Equi than a firm selected solely by Apollo's industry, employee count and keywords.

## The build

Allocator Intelligence is an independent research workbench. It imports a real Apollo sample, separates firmographic fit from opportunity evidence, penalizes documented competition, keeps unknowns visible, and produces review-only outreach angles. It can challenge its own first score as new sources arrive.

## What we learned

- 6,173 companies were initially matched; 2,412 survived filtering. 25 underwent first-pass research. These are research funnel sizes, not conversion rates.
- Eight firms received 100/100 ICP fit. Multiple were later shown to have competitive or structural complexities.
- Maridea's Form ADV documents use of independent managers and roughly $1.97B in client assets. It's a research lead, not proven buyer intent.
- Dynasty's Allocate partnership is a substantial competitive objection for overlapping white-label private markets propositions.
- Gresham describes access to outside managers while already operating client-specific private partnership structures.

## What success would require

Obtain authorization from Equi, define a narrow allocator ICP, compare evidence-based outreach to matched generic outreach, require compliance review, and measure qualified meetings rather than AI-generated account scores. Treat the experiment as exploratory until results are observed.

## Honest scope

This is a functional offline v1.0 with deterministic triage and data operations. It is not yet a live autonomous agent, and it has never sent outreach or generated attributable pipeline. No endorsement by Equi is implied.
